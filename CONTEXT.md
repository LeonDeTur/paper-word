# Personal article and vocabulary library

The service connects reading English articles with learning the words and phrases found in them.

## Domain language

**Article**: English material with a title, source and content, added to a personal library.

**Article translation**: Russian text paired with the article's original content. It can be imported or generated with AI.

**Block**: part of the original article paired with a translation, such as a paragraph.

**Vocabulary card**: an entry for learning an English word or phrase, with its translation and usage context.

**Usage context**: the original article block from which the user saved a word or phrase.

**Library**: the collection of articles and translations added by the user.

**Base form**: the canonical spelling of a word or phrase. For example, `observed` becomes `observe`, and `took into account` becomes `take into account`.

**Review direction**: a way to test one vocabulary entry, from English to Russian or Russian to English. Each direction has independent results and a next-review date.

**Review**: one attempt to recall a card's answer in a selected direction, followed by a difficulty rating.
